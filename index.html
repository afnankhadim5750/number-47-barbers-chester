02 — The signature finish

The hot towel
is the point.

Anyone can take a bit off the top. The finish is where a barbershop earns its regulars. Ask for the hot towel and neck shave and the chair reclines, the towel goes on warm, and the cut gets the slow ending it deserves.

  • Hot towel and neck shave on request
  • Traditional wet shave with a straight razor
  • Beard work finished in the same sitting

03 — The cut journey

From the door
to the door.

Three steps, no shortcuts. This is how a visit to Number 47 runs.

  1. 01

    The consult

    You say what you want — and what you don’t. The barber listens first, then picks up the clippers.

  2. 02

    The cut

    Scissors, clippers and comb — worked through properly, checked in the mirror as it takes shape.

  3. 03

    The hot-towel finish

    Hot towel, neck shave and a final tidy — the part regulars come back for. Walk out sharp.

04 — The shop

Boughton’s
barbershop
since 2014.

Number 47 has been cutting hair on Christleton Road since 2014. Run by directors Ollie Foster and Lynne Foster, it’s built its name the slow way — one good cut at a time, and regulars who keep coming back.

The shop is modern and welcoming, the atmosphere is brilliant, and the chat is part of the service. Freshly brewed hot and cold drinks are always available while you wait.
